## Welcome

This is the first post on the new site.

## How it works

The blog is powered by Markdown files in `content/blog`. Create a new `.md` file with front matter for `title`, `description`, and `date`, then it will appear on the blog page automatically.

### Writing a new post

Add headings to structure your article. They are automatically included in the table of contents.
