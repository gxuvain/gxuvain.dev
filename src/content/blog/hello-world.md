## Welcome

This is the first post on the new site.

## How it works

The blog is powered by Markdown files in `src/content/blog`. Create a new `.md` file with frontmatter for its title, description, author, date, and cover image, then it will appear on the blog page automatically.

### Writing a new post

Add headings to structure your article and links to share useful resources.
